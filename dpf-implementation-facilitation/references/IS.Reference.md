## IS.Reference:1 - Implementation facilitation and the language that describes it

Implementation facilitation is the practical work of helping practitioners, organizations and affected people make an evidence-informed practice usable in their circumstances, examine its consequences and improve their ability to conduct subsequent changes. The facilitator works on that connection. The service practitioner performs the target practice; its recipient experiences its benefit, burden or exclusion; the responsible authority decides matters within its remit. One person may occupy several of these positions, but an agreement to facilitate does not supply another position's competence or authority.

This Architectural Reference addresses users of the framework: facilitators, the people receiving their help, and those deciding what help to provide. It explains what the Methods obtain, why they are connected, and where another practice must supply a result. The main sources concern human and social services. Transfer to a professional community, independent practice or an AI-assisted arrangement requires the actual recipient, intervention, authority and evidence conditions to be recovered. It is not an effectiveness conclusion inherited from those sources.

The field combines knowledge about how a proposed practice works in a setting with reusable ways of helping its participants change that practice. Research evidence, professional judgement and the experience of people affected constrain different claims; disagreement among them is a reason to examine those claims, not to average them into consensus. Facilitation selects and adapts a response, participants enact the changed work, and observations return to the practice account and its facilitating Methods. The whole can be judged by how well its combined knowledge and facilitating practices enable a justified, usable change in the receiving setting, and by how observations from that change improve later action. To examine this, recover why the change was selected, what participants actually did, what happened to the receiving work, and what the result permits next.

Different evidence models, facilitation strategies and provider arrangements can serve this field when they preserve that connection and its limits. A new target profession, a different organizational home or a changed tool does not by itself create another field. Replacing the purpose with research alone, direct service delivery alone or unrestricted organizational management changes the promised whole. Implementation research remains a source and a possible receiver of qualified observations; this framework does not absorb its complete research practice. Organizational change, human development and community management likewise keep their own subjects.

One possible larger field assembly is implementation science and practice: connecting warranted practice knowledge with its use in receiving settings and returning the consequences to inquiry. Evidence synthesis or translation makes the relied-on practice account and its limits usable; implementation research investigates how delivery, mechanisms and context affect results; the target profession supplies actual delivery and its professional judgement. Facilitation connects those contributions to an obtainable change with the people affected. Their combined contribution is the ability to obtain a justified, usable implementation and revise its grounds from receiving evidence. Neither a sound research account nor successful delivery alone supplies that whole answer.

Within that possible assembly, this same facilitation field receives qualified practice accounts and relevant knowledge of recipients and settings. It supplies adapted, obtainable help and bounded observations of what was delivered, used or prevented. Those observations can challenge a relied-on assumption or identify a research question; the receiving research or synthesis practice determines what broader conclusion they support. Different professions, evidence models or providers preserve the facilitation contribution when this knowledge–help–observation connection, the recipient's practice, and the limits of professional judgement and authority remain. Replacing the helping contribution with research alone or direct delivery changes that constituent. Select the contributions needed by the undertaking.

The language has two useful levels. IS.1–IS.3 and IS.5 explain agreement, diagnosis, strategy and continuation of help. The general Core Method [FPF A.3.1.MI](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#a31mi---introduce-a-method-into-practice-and-decide-what-to-keep-or-change) supplies introduction and interpretation of later use, including an implementer's direct use without a helping relationship. The broader applicability of these Methods does not make every instance implementation facilitation. IS.6–IS.8 develop three recurring facilitation constructions: make cross-party knowledge affect a practice despite unequal influence; act on the interaction of the proposed practice, participants and context; and develop the recipients' ability to facilitate another change. The latter construction uses human-development and organization-capability Methods.

For an uncomplicated access failure, use IS.2 or [FPF A.15.8.MS](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#a158ms---configure-methodological-support-for-work) and stop when the missing contribution is supplied. For a disagreement whose resolution depends on who can affect the practice, use IS.6. For a practice that must change with its setting, use IS.7. For recipients expected to lead a later change, use IS.8. Existing adequate results can enter any of these Methods directly.

This field is narrower than all implementation practice and wider than chairing a discussion. A facilitation-only meeting can be enough when discussion is the missing contribution. An undertaking involving evidence interpretation, changed provision, protected participation and later capability needs the corresponding connected work. Obtain the required contributions from the relevant practices: [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) compares whole arrangements, [OCE.6](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/DPFs/Management%20DPF%20Suite/ORGANIZATION-CHANGE-ENGINEERING-PRINCIPLES-FRAMEWORK.md#oce6---establish-holder-assignments-and-enabling-relations-for-organization-change) supplies effective organizational relations, and the target profession supplies its technical judgement. A common office, a specialist, an internal practitioner, an external partner and distributed provision remain alternatives.

Implementation can finish while development continues. A useful result may expose a more demanding attainable problem, a changed aim or a missing Method. [FPF C.40.OD](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c40od---construct-and-continue-open-ended-development) constructs the development opportunity, [FPF C.40.JD](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c40jd---develop-a-way-its-holder-and-its-provision-together) relates changes in the way, its holder and its provision, and [FPF C.40.NP](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c40np---continue-from-a-result-to-new-attainable-problems) continues from a result to another worthwhile problem. Facilitation helps a selected change become useful.

## IS.Reference:2 - Make useful practice obtainable in real work

Use the general helping Methods in this framework when people or systems are expected to use a practice, but a description, invitation, tool or training event has not made the intended work obtainable. A practice may be new to its recipients, a familiar way that has become difficult to continue, or a proposed way whose local fitness remains under examination. The support practitioner helps obtain the contributions that make its use possible and worthwhile, examines what actually happens, and changes or ends the help accordingly.

Start with one receiving use. Ask the performer to show what they are trying to obtain, the occasion in which they need it and the first contribution they cannot obtain. A missing permission may need an authorized decision; a difficult judgement may need an expert; a missing personal ability may need practice and feedback; an unsuitable method may need revision or replacement. The first useful result can be one repaired connection.

Within implementation facilitation, providing and revising help changes a condition for establishing, restoring, adapting or continuing a recipient's practice. It does not cover every professional service merely because that service supplies a useful result. If an adequate service already makes the wanted result obtainable, use it directly. A helper can arrange qualified performance as part of a support strategy without requiring the recipient to acquire that specialty; arranging the support and performing the target operation remain different contributions. The field also differs from designing every part of the organization or choosing the recipient's whole development strategy.

The practitioner needs to understand the proposed support operations, recover the receiving work with its participants, and obtain qualified subject judgement where the decision depends on it. They need not personally master every supported profession. A source, expert or capable AI can supply a contribution, but its availability, fitness and permission to act remain conditions of the proposed arrangement.

## IS.Reference:3 - Keep the target, the help and their results distinct

The **target practice** is what the receiving performers are trying to do. The **support strategy** is the proposed way of helping them establish, use or revise it. The **support arrangement** supplies the people, systems, time, tools, access and decisions needed for that strategy. A **support undertaking** is the bounded work and commitments through which that help is provided.

These distinctions change action. A different explanation of a target method may repair its description without changing the method. Replacing a classroom session with rehearsal changes the support strategy. Repairing an unavailable reference changes the arrangement. Changing the target's decision criterion can change the method itself. A successful result does not settle which change made it possible.

The receiving promise also matters:

| Wanted result | What must actually be obtained |
| --- | --- |
| A useful result from the target practice | The result and conditions the receiver needs, including qualified external performance when that is the chosen way. |
| A person's independent or appropriately supported ability | That person's contribution in representative work under the promised support conditions, using the applicable human-development and assessment methods. |
| A continuing organizational contribution | The combined work, effective relations, means and ordinary provision conditions that allow the organization to deliver it. |
| Ability to arrange later implementation | The intended holder's performance of noticing a difficulty, obtaining suitable help, making permitted decisions and using the result, at the promised scope. |
| Continuation among other carriers | Obtainable material and support, actual receiving uses and the particular cultural relations claimed; publication or a first successful handover is insufficient for a population-wide claim. |

Selection, construction and adaptation of the target practice can remain open. For a particular attempt, recover the intended contribution, the operations actually to be performed, their conditions and the result that can change the next decision. Preserve the status of a proposed method account. A.3.1.MI explains the stronger Method, Work and evidence distinctions when the use relies on them.

If there is no useful target question yet, open inquiry rather than invent an implementation plan. B.5.PI and C.40.CD help form and revise questions; C.40.OD helps construct and compare development opportunities; C.39 and the relevant subject practice develop the obtaining way. Return to implementation support when its help can make a consequential difference. Continued development is wider than successful implementation and can open another worthwhile problem after this undertaking ends.

## IS.Reference:4 - Use the connected Methods from the contribution that is missing

IS.1 makes the support undertaking intelligible and workable. IS.2 locates the consequential difficulty and obtains the next useful contribution. IS.3 constructs the actual support strategy and its provision. A.3.1.MI performs and examines a bounded introduction, keeping target and support changes separate. IS.5 obtains continuing use, a qualified transfer, expansion or a responsible end. IS.6 makes knowledge from differently influential parties change the implementation; IS.7 acts on the practice–participants–context interaction; IS.8 develops recipients' subsequent facilitation work. A Method can use another's result where the work requires it.

A sufficient current arrangement can enter at A.3.1.MI. An inherited service can require only an IS.5 handover. A failed attempt can return directly to IS.2 or to the subject method's owner. Existing evidence can settle a question without another trial. Follow an ordinary path only while its next contribution is needed.

**Long working mantra**

> Recover the receiving result and who must obtain it; agree what help is wanted and permitted; follow a real use to the consequential difficulty; distinguish the explanations that change action; obtain or construct the needed contribution; compare and provide appropriate help with its recipients; perform the bounded introduction and examine later work; separate changes to the target, the help and the conditions; revise the exact unsupported claim; preserve, transfer, extend or end support on the basis of what continued use really requires; return a changed developmental aim or an unavailable subject method to the practice that can settle it.

The return connections matter as much as the forward ones. Diagnosis may reveal that the original request promised the wrong result. A good target can require different support. A useful local adaptation can become a method-development question. A successful episode can reveal an unsustainable dependence. The practitioner follows the affected contribution rather than restarting every activity.

## IS.Reference:5 - One performer, several practices; several performers, one result

A local leader, internal specialist, external provider, independent practitioner or qualified AI can perform implementation-support work. Several parties can contribute under their own organizational arrangements and decision authority.

One residency coordinator, for example, can develop a method during a development project and later organize programmes and cohorts as ordinary provision. Project membership, method of work, capability and organizational assignment are different relations.

The target operation still needs its actual subject expertise. The same facilitator can help two professions investigate a difficulty without being qualified to decide both professions' technical questions. Conversely, a sufficiently capable AI can supply a previously scarce contribution. Permissions, physical access, reliable evidence and the intended holder's own performance remain separate questions.

Use C.32.MWA.PP when materially different configurations of performance, Method co-use and provision must be compared. Its selected configuration and conditions feed the support strategy; proposed relations still need realization. A.15.8.MS supplies method-use support, and C.32.MWA distinguishes the interacting structures where that changes the comparison. Obtain OCE and Operations results for organization, allocation and continuing service. Use their sufficient results directly, without a second comparison or introduction.

## IS.Reference:6 - Recognize a useful local result before claiming a larger one

A plan, delivered workshop, configured tool, attempted use and successful receiving result are different findings. A record of activity can be complete while a required condition is missing. Begin with the strongest useful conclusion the actual grounds support.

For ordinary help, a short account can suffice: the difficulty, the contribution supplied, what the recipient could do with it, what remains conditional and the next justified action. Use more precise models or stronger evidence when the decision depends on them. A capability claim needs the intended holder and work conditions. A cultural claim needs the actual carriers and relations. A causal claim needs its own C.28 qualification.

C.11.DUA compares what an attainable answer could change with the work, delay and interruption required to obtain it. Preserve a robust bounded response when live explanations do not change it. A mandatory protection condition remains binding until changed by its actual authority.

## IS.Reference:7 - Sources, alternatives and limits

The Methods connect implementation help to actual work and to the professional contributions on which it depends. A generic catalogue of barriers or strategies can suggest a question, but cannot determine the operation, provider, timing or appropriate result by itself. A target-specific rollout recipe can be enough in a stable setting; use it when it supplies the whole needed contribution. Reopen the support construction when conditions, recipients or the target change.

Implementation-support literature develops a helping practice. The [2020 conceptual account](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-020-05145-1) and [Practice Guide to Supporting Implementation](https://implementation.fpg.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/ISP-Practice-Guide.pdf) emphasize tailored help and building recipients' implementation capacity in service systems. They distinguish that help from taking over delivery. Here, recipient participation, contextual judgement, actual use and feedback are retained. Continued qualified expert or AI supply is also an option when the receiving practice needs that contribution but personal acquisition is not the promise. This extension depends on the actual provider, capability, permission and receiving-use conditions; it is not an effectiveness result inherited from human-service research. A claim that an AI system's capability changed needs evidence for that system under the stated conditions.

A.3.1.MI supplies a general introduction Method: an implementer can use it directly without a separate helping relationship. Its wider independent use supports this language without making every implementation activity an instance of support. It distinguishes implementation observations, target adaptation, support-strategy adaptation and causal explanation. HCD, OCE, CB and the Core Method-development family remain suppliers of their complete Methods. The constructed cases illustrate reasoning and operations, not empirical effectiveness.

This framework does not supply each target profession's technical Method, a universal learning theory, every organization's design or a general development strategy. It tells the support practitioner how to obtain and use the needed contribution, keep its conditions intact, and return the work when it cannot be supplied. A provider should reduce a promise that depends on unavailable expertise.

The short entry routes foreground the receiving work, helper contribution and next decision. They omit the detailed technical, learning and organizational constructions supplied by the pattern bodies and the external practices in IS.Reference:1. Return to those bodies when the shorter account leaves an operation, condition or evidence claim unresolved. The examples illustrate selected ways of combining the Methods.

The links locate the supplying publications; for a reproducible use, identify the edition or revision actually read. Their PatternIDs locate the relied-on bodies. Read the relevant Method and its conditions when you must perform it; use a sufficient existing result directly when its applicability and actual availability are already established. If a linked body or required expertise cannot be obtained, preserve the dependent limit instead of assuming it has been supplied.

## IS.Reference:9 - Reconsider only the contribution whose basis has changed

Use the following differences to locate a needed return:

| Changed basis | Contribution to reconsider |
| --- | --- |
| The recipient's wanted result, authority or service terms change. | The IS.1 undertaking and any dependent support strategy. |
| A formerly sufficient explanation no longer distinguishes action-changing cases. | The IS.2 diagnosis and its subject or research supplier. |
| A provider, tool, source, permission, workload or relevant recipient condition changes. | IS.3 provision and adaptation; A.15.8.MS, HCD or OCE where the needed contribution belongs. |
| Later work contradicts a target, strategy, fit or causal claim. | A.3.1.MI's exact maintained claim and the professional return that can revise it. |
| Continued use depends on help that is ending or on a new receiving setting. | The IS.5 continuation, transfer or extension. |
| Better implementation-support knowledge changes the explanation or useful operation. | The affected helping Method and its examples, source bounds and related returns. |
| A relied-on FPF or DPF meaning changes. | The affected dependency and the IS instructions that consume it, before relying on unchanged wording. |

Recover which claim has lost its basis and what current contribution would repair the receiving use. Reuse unaffected knowledge and qualified results. Where the change exposes a larger development question, return it at that scope.

## IS.Reference:10 - What framework quality does and does not establish

[FPF E.21](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e21---fpf-pattern-quality-evaluation-characteristicspace) supplies judgement of a pattern's contribution, while [FPF E.4.DPF.DA](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#e4dpfda---evaluate-a-dpf-or-lpf-package-for-its-declared-use-package-adequacy-characteristicspace) concerns this connected framework against its declared practice. [FPF F.19](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#f19---ontology-first-plain-technical-rewriting) addresses wording that prevents recovery of an action, participant or condition. A good local result, a readable explanation and a justified source use answer different questions; each result supports only its own scope. These framework-quality judgements explain what the publication warrants.

For a local support strategy, the actual target, help and receiving result are evaluated through [FPF A.3.1.MI](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#a31mi---introduce-a-method-into-practice-and-decide-what-to-keep-or-change) and the appropriate professional practice.

## IS.Reference:11 - Supplier availability and independent changes

A practitioner can use a sufficient result from a supplier directly; an inaccessible or inapplicable supplier leaves the dependent result unavailable. Changed evidence can alter the target practice, the facilitating response or the developmental arrangement independently.

For source qualifications, citation and edition information, see the [Sources and Edition Notes](#implementation-facilitation-principles-framework-sources-and-edition-notes).

The [current source questions](#issources2---follow-the-current-source-questions) identify the source contributions and changes relevant to these availability judgements.
