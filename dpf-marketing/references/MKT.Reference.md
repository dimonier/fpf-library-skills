# MKT.Reference: MKT.Reference:1 - Problem frame - Useful offers in actual customer situations

> **Problem frame:** Marketing examines and changes the relations through which people discover, choose, obtain and use offerings, and through which providers learn what to offer and how to sustain that provision.
> **First move:** Some connections use an earlier result: qualification informs an offer, and evidence from a performed trial informs a later choice. Others are simultaneous.

---

## MKT.Reference:1 - Problem frame - Useful offers in actual customer situations

Marketing examines and changes the relations through which people discover, choose, obtain and use offerings, and through which providers learn what to offer and how to sustain that provision. Its practical object is larger than a message or a sale: a useful contribution has to connect a customer's circumstances, a provider's capabilities, a meaningful choice and actual provision. Marketing knowledge concerns these relations and their conditions; marketing practice investigates them and constructs, performs and revises ways of working with them.

This language develops that account through customer and market inquiry, offers and positioning, communication and advertising arrangements, acquisition, experience, relationships, commercial distribution and repeatable customer work. A founder, service team, learning provider or business-to-business programme owner can enter with one bounded problem. The reader can investigate the domain, work with customers and obtain engineering, operating or economic contributions when needed. The language does not supply every specialised method of brand strategy, price research, media buying, logistics or public policy. It explains how their required contributions enter the customer work and when a result must return to its responsible practice.

Use the language when misunderstanding a customer's work, overextending evidence, disconnecting a promise from provision or confusing one kind of result with another could change the next decision. A known suitable offer with a workable ordinary purchase may need only its existing operating method. A personal learning problem, a community mandate, an engineering design or a liquidity decision keeps its own professional owner even when commercial work depends on it.

### MKT.Reference:1.1 - Form the objects from the work

Begin with an episode in which somebody is trying to obtain a result. Recover what the person is doing, what makes the result useful, what currently happens, what alternatives are available and which conditions matter. A requested product feature is one possible means. A difficulty completing the person's work can survive its delivery. MKT.12 investigates the consequential question; MKT.1 constructs the customer problem and the differences between situations that would change a decision.

The people who use, choose, pay, permit, maintain and bear consequences can be different. These are relations to a particular activity, not permanent kinds of people. A team member can use a service while an employer pays; a retailer can buy goods upstream and sell them downstream. Identify the organisation or person and the actual relation whenever a claim about “the customer” would otherwise change meaning. A role description alone supplies neither a holder nor the authority, capability or time to perform its work.

Form a customer group from differences relevant to the intended decision. For an offer decision, ask whether one proposal remains suitable across the included situations under the stated conditions; a relevant difference can require separate treatment. Investigate cases that could defeat that grouping. A market account then relates such customer situations to available alternatives, providers, access conditions and exchanges over a stated place and period. A list of contacts, an advertising audience and the people who can both choose and obtain the contribution may cover different populations.

Construct an offer by connecting the intended result to a contribution a provider can supply, the recipient's required work, consideration, conditions and continuation. MKT.2 obtains these parts and tests their uncertain connections. An opportunity exists for the present work when a particular situation could support a worthwhile continuation; interest alone does not qualify its conditions. A customer programme becomes useful when several such continuations share consequential preparation or resources. MKT.14 constructs that larger arrangement without erasing their separate decisions and obligations.

These distinctions allow the same statement to be tested at the right object. “They want it” may report expressed interest; “they can buy it” requires a possible choice and acquisition; “it is available” requires provision under the relevant conditions; “it helps” concerns use and benefit. Retain the differences that can change what to investigate, offer, promise or perform.

### MKT.Reference:1.2 - Connect the functions without prescribing departments or a funnel

Work backwards from useful use to the contributions it requires, and forwards from each proposed contribution to its receiving activity. A person may need to discover an unfamiliar alternative, compare it, obtain permission, acquire access, prepare for use and receive help. Another person may already know exactly what to buy. The first situation can need several kinds of work; the second can need only a functioning ordinary purchase. Their relationship is conditional, not a compulsory sequence of stages.

Marketing contributes the investigation and construction that connects these activities: which situation to serve, what useful difference to offer, how people can encounter and judge it, and what arrangements make it obtainable and sustainable. In an advertising arrangement, a sponsor obtains a medium's contribution to presenting a message. The media work determines available contexts, expressions, placement controls and observations. MKT.3 and the intermediary application connect that contribution to the supported offer and the work receiving a response. Exposure supplies an occasion for attention; it does not supply the customer's decision.

Sales work helps a particular customer compare, decide and complete an acquisition on usable terms. MKT.4 distinguishes interpretation, suitability, authority, concern and transition because each can require a different response. In a major account, choosing criteria, negotiating a package and enabling implementation are consequential constructions. In an ordinary repeat purchase, those questions may already be settled. A commercial agreement can establish obligations while leaving the work, access or capability needed to fulfil them absent.

Distribution makes the offering obtainable where, when and on the conditions that the intended use requires. It can include selecting intermediaries, locating availability, allocating selling and support work, arranging transfer or access, and connecting orders, payment, information, returns and recovery. Physical delivery is one possible contribution. A digital service also needs an effective route to authorised access and support. MKT.11 connects these commercial requirements to an actual provider arrangement; MKT.2 and MKT.4 receive the resulting offer and acquisition conditions.

A channel therefore needs its object stated. The route by which somebody encounters a claim can differ from the route through which they buy and receive the contribution. One platform can provide both; one retailer can advertise, sell, hold stock and support use. Recover the actual relations instead of assigning all these functions from its name. The provider and a merchant intermediary have one exchange; the intermediary and its customer can have another. Each exchange needs its own offer, feasible performance and grounds for interpreting observations.

These functions need not become separate departments, firms or levels of a system. One person can perform several, and several organisations can supply one. Decide that arrangement from the required results, authority, capability, timing and conflicts in the work. Marketing also depends on engineering, economics, operations, learning and community practice. Their contributions retain their own objects and conditions; commercial importance does not turn their Methods into sales techniques.

### MKT.Reference:1.3 - Ground knowledge and change ways of working

A useful account explains what is known, how it was obtained and what decision it supports. A customer's account can reveal a purpose, interpretation or reported difficulty. Observation can establish that an activity or event occurred. A provider can answer for a capability or commitment within its authority. Calculation can expose consequences under stated assumptions. A causal conclusion needs evidence and assumptions that distinguish the intervention's contribution from competing explanations. MKT.12 obtains a qualified answer; MKT.10 handles the causal and full-cost comparison when the decision requires it.

A reusable method needs more than a successful instance. Explain the relation that made the result possible and the conditions on which it depended. For example, local availability helps an urgent replenishment need only if the right item is present, can be identified and can be obtained in time. More exposure cannot supply missing stock. An assisted trial can support meaningful choice only if the promised assistance and required customer participation are available. Its purchase count cannot by itself establish the participant's later competence or benefit.

Develop alternatives from what the situation leaves unresolved. MKT.6 can help construct a useful result; MKT.2 compares proposed contributions and positioning; MKT.9 obtains or constructs a fitting variant; MKT.11 obtains the necessary joint provision. Use existing adequate results directly. Where no supported arrangement exists, engineering constructs alternatives with the receiving use and provider conditions together. Economic reasoning compares exchanges and consequential terms; the relevant authority establishes permissions. Select an arrangement by the whole result and burden, then obtain the decisions and work that make it effective.

Practice returns evidence to the account. A failed acquisition may expose an inaccessible payment route rather than an unattractive offer. A successful sale followed by unused access can expose missing preparation, a changed purpose or an unsuitable contribution. Investigate the difference before generalising. It may require a local remedy, a changed method, a revised customer group or a narrower claim about where the method works. Repeated use and maintained counterexamples improve the reusable account; repeating the same procedure does not establish its generality.

The purpose of an intervention also remains open to judgement. Provider return, customer benefit, voluntary participation, learning and effects on others are distinct concerns. Evidence that a technique increases purchase cannot decide whether its burden, disclosure, permission or treatment of refusal is acceptable. The ethical distinctions in §6 constrain the alternatives throughout this work.

### MKT.Reference:1.4 - Return the whole account to different situations

Consider a few changes that require different continuations. A known consumable needed today makes verified availability and a workable purchase decisive. If the local seller loses stock, MKT.11's changed supply answer returns to MKT.2's promise and MKT.3's invitation; buying more attention cannot preserve “available today”. A complex service with several decision makers can instead require MKT.4's comparison and negotiation, followed by actual assignments and access. When permission is missing, another explanation of the already understood benefit supplies no substitute.

A platform that changes which parts of a message it can omit changes the advertising arrangement. The intermediary application returns to the family of possible meanings and actual controls before choosing a route. A learning offer raises another relation: the buyer's agreement, the learner's participation and acquired capability are different results. The learning application obtains HCD and community contributions where needed and allows an informed refusal or a separate personal route.

Finally, success in a few supported cases can leave ordinary repetition unresolved. MKT.13 asks what acquisition, provision, benefit and economics depend on, including exceptional help. MKT.14 may make common preparation worthwhile, but a new organisation's access restriction or a competing specialist obligation can defeat the larger programme. Return that fact to the whole comparison and revise the affected promises. These returns are how a connected marketing account changes decisions in its parts.

## MKT.Reference:2 - Problem and forces - Connect usefulness, choice and provision

A company can attract attention without helping a customer act. It can deliver a requested feature without improving the result that matters to that customer. It can also construct a useful offer that its own organisation cannot sustain. Each local success leaves a different unresolved question. Treating them all as a conversion problem invites more persuasion where changed provision or an agreed refusal is needed.

Several forces shape the answer. Customers differ in their work, circumstances, authority and available help, while preparation is costly and some of it can be shared. A clear promise aids choice but can create reliance before provision is established. A timely decision needs a bounded inquiry, while a causal or population claim may require more evidence than that decision does. Current service, new exploration and learning often compete for the same people. A common programme must respect those simultaneous demands as well as its total cost.

The language keeps these differences usable. It supports an adequate small answer, construction when no suitable option exists, and a larger programme when several interdependent opportunities warrant it. Customer usefulness, learning, voluntary participation, provider solvency and commercial return require distinct questions and evidence.

## MKT.Reference:4 - Composition and performance - Preserve the conditions of the whole

Some connections use an earlier result: qualification informs an offer, and evidence from a performed trial informs a later choice. Others are simultaneous. During a customer meeting, a facilitator may be eliciting a useful result, helping the person understand an offer and performing part of a larger programme. The same worker may also be responsible for an existing service. Those descriptions identify different contributions and constraints of the work occurring then. Account for the shared worker’s time against the whole programme.

For a proposed combination, name one intended result, the included opportunities, the period and the permitted conditions. Identify the preparation, capability, assignments, access, performance, support, coordination, updating and exception handling that the result would require, retaining any unresolved supply conditions. Count a shared contribution once where it is actually shared; count local work for each case that needs it. Preserve different resource kinds and time windows. Compare complete ways of producing a comparable result, including a smaller or later programme where the original promise cannot be supported. If implementation is selected, obtain the effective arrangements needed before dependent work begins.

Apply this whole-combination condition to the applications below. An apparently cheaper message can increase the number of unsupported responses. A common demonstration can save preparation while increasing coordination. An AI-generated explanation can reduce drafting time while leaving a subject judgement, an individual learning action or a timely human check unsupplied. Inspect those differences before replacing one constituent in a larger arrangement. [FPF C.32.MWA.PP](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c32mwapp---construct-and-compare-method-performance-and-provision-arrangements) develops the method and performance comparisons; engineering, organisation and operations supply the actual arrangements.

The four-trial calculation in [MKT.13](#mkt13---test-commercial-model-repeatability-and-deployment-limits) and the 44/40-hour comparison in [MKT.14](#mkt14---develop-and-change-a-programme-for-selected-customer-organisations) are deliberately separate constructed situations. In the latter, three organisations contain four independently changing opportunities. Keep the unit of work and the recipient of each result explicit.

## MKT.Reference:5 - Recognition and assurance - Know what the evidence establishes

Recognising a situation selects a potentially useful method. Establishing its result requires the appropriate work and evidence. A stated difficulty may justify a clarifying conversation without establishing its prevalence. A feasible prototype can justify a bounded trial without establishing willingness to buy. A paid trial can demonstrate an acquisition without establishing sustained benefit or ordinary repeatability.

Give each consequential conclusion its object, source, conditions and limit. A customer's account, an observed action, a provider commitment, a calculation and a causal estimate answer different questions. MKT.12 determines sufficient inquiry for a decision. MKT.10 uses causal modelling and identification when an effect claim is required, then carries uncertainty into the economic comparison. MKT.13 asks whether the ordinary arrangement can repeat and sustain the work at a named scale. None of these results supplies a missing permission or allocation.

For a learning offer, human performance under stated help conditions belongs to HCD; satisfaction and purchase are different observations. For community work, attendance can describe reach while leaving useful exchange and the burden of organising unresolved. The constructed applications show how to reason about these distinctions. They are neither observed success cases nor evidence of the effectiveness of the framework as a whole.

## MKT.Reference:6 - Bias-Annotation - Recover whose result and whose burden

The paying organisation, buyer, user, learner, referrer, organiser and provider can have different purposes and authority. Ask whose result is being described and whose account is absent. A manager can fund a programme without determining what a worker personally wants to learn; a community host can invite discussion without promising its participants as sales prospects.

People who declined, could not gain access, needed unusual help or left early may reveal conditions that successful participants conceal. Include their relevant circumstances when the receiving question requires them. Avoid extrapolating from the customers easiest to contact or serve. An average can hide both an unserved group and a scarce contribution that makes the average possible.

Evidence access and disclosure themselves have conditions. Reuse a result only for the permitted purpose and adequate population. Obtain an appropriate anonymised example or customer-local continuation when sharing identifiable material is unavailable. These are working conditions to resolve with the relevant holders, not assumptions granted by a common programme.

## MKT.Reference:7 - Conformance and common failures - Follow the intended use

Before relying on a connected result, follow one real or representative use from the opening question to the receiving action. Can the recipient understand the input, perform the needed operation and act on the returned result? Identify where a changed condition requires another decision. Use the following questions where they can change that reliance:

- Is the useful result distinguished from the requested product, communication response and provider revenue?
- Do the customer roles and differences change a real choice, rather than merely rename market segments?
- Does the offer retain the conditions of its engineering, learning or operating supply?
- Are the particular promise, recipient, permissions and next performer available?
- Does the evidence support the actual claim, population, conditions and time horizon being used?
- Can the whole programme fit its consequential resources and timing while keeping existing obligations?
- Can an affected person decline, defer, obtain help, correct a mistake or end the arrangement on understandable terms?

Several errors are invited by familiar marketing shorthand. A funnel can turn every pause into a sales failure even when the person has obtained useful help. A persona can substitute a stereotype for differences that actually change the offer. An experience concept can become a theme unrelated to work the provider supplies. Using one account score can make the team treat every opportunity as advanced after one contact. Correct the specific inference: recover the person's work, the actual difference, the supplied contribution or the independently changing opportunity. The direct bodies develop each correction and its ordinary stopping point.

## MKT.Reference:8 - Consequences - What changes in practice?

The team can make a smaller justified promise, obtain a missing contribution, stop an unnecessary inquiry, or decline an unsuitable programme before creating unsupported reliance. Customer knowledge can change actual provision. Shared preparation can be used where it remains adequate, with exceptions and continuing service included in the comparison. A negative or uncertain result remains useful when it changes the next decision.

This work also costs time. Jointly shaping a result, qualifying evidence, obtaining commitments and supporting exceptions can be more expensive than producing campaign materials. Use existing qualified results and direct local methods where sufficient. Deeper construction is warranted when it changes the available offer, its assurance or the whole burden.

The descriptions themselves have an obtaining cost: learning the language, locating a method, adapting it, checking an AI contribution and maintaining the explanation. Compare that cost over a named range of uses if it matters to adoption. Count common development once and repeated human work where it recurs.

## MKT.Reference:9 - Architectural Rationale - A repertoire with connected results

A single chronological sales sequence would be easy to display but would conceal returning, parallel and noncommercial work. A list of independent tactics would preserve choice while leaving practitioners to invent the connections. This edition instead provides separately usable methods with explicit receiving results and shared applications. It allows a local answer without requiring every body, while developing the connections that make a larger answer possible.

Research and qualification remain distinct from offer construction because new evidence can change the problem without producing a solution. Offer positioning, experience design and execution remain distinct because a meaningful promise, a desired experience and available provision can fail separately. Customer success and continuation remain distinct from purchase because useful change and remaining obligations outlive the transaction. Intervention evaluation remains distinct from repeatability because a supported effect in one setting does not establish a sustainable commercial arrangement elsewhere.

MKT.14 owns construction of a programme for selected customer organisations. Its contribution is to combine shared preparation, individual opportunities and whole provision under change. A conventional single-customer enquiry can use MKT.1/2/4 without it. An advertising platform or customer database may assist the programme but does not establish the programme's useful result, permissible commonality or receiving work.

Specialised contributions stay with their owners. Marketing can identify a missing service and consume a constructed variant without supplying systems engineering. It can frame the commercial question of an experiment without owning the organisation of search. It can offer education without defining how human capability develops, and it can take part in community activity without making that activity a stage of acquisition. These boundaries preserve usable differences when several methods address the same event.

## MKT.Reference:10 - SoTA-Echoing - Preserve distinct answers to distinct questions

The source discussions in each body's §11 state the adopted or adapted contribution and its limits. Together they support the following choices.

Customer research does not have one interchangeable Jobs-to-be-Done method. Inquiry into progress and switching and analysis of a job's desired outcomes can ask different questions, use different units and support different decisions. MKT.12 selects the line from the receiving question; MKT.1 qualifies its customer differences. Positioning then uses relevant alternatives, distinctive capabilities and value under the customer's circumstances. It is not a substitute for investigating whether the proposed contribution is useful.

Experience work contributes both the construction of an intended experience and attention to what actually occurs in use. MKT.5 retains that distinction; MKT.8 uses a journey map to expose relevant connections without manufacturing one compulsory route. Historical experience-economy and modular-service treatments are used for their specified constructions in MKT.5/9, with later experience and customer-success research informing the limits on what a provider can claim. Experience during use, engagement and useful change remain distinguishable.

Customer-success work makes the useful outcome and enabling participation consequential before and after purchase. MKT.6 adapts this contribution to joint goal formation and help; MKT.7 applies it to continuation and ending. A provider's success metric cannot settle the customer's own goal.

Commercial search and account-based work contribute different ways to investigate, organise and repeat customer work. MKT.13 preserves the difference between search and ordinary repeatable delivery. MKT.14 retains both individual programmes and justified common work, including their preparation and exception costs. Automation changes some work and introduces other work; it does not erase the difference between a contact signal and a qualified opportunity.

For causal questions, MKT.10 adopts the distinction between describing observations, identifying an effect under justified assumptions, estimating it and using it in a prospective choice. A fitted model, a before/after difference and a complete economic comparison are separate contributions. Reopen the affected inference when the population, intervention, supply conditions or relied-on source changes.

The [American Marketing Association's 2017 definition](https://www.ama.org/the-definition-of-marketing-what-is-marketing/) supplies a broad field boundary including creation, communication, delivery and exchange. That boundary does not supply the Methods for doing those things. The channel treatment in [OpenStax, Principles of Marketing, §§17.1–17.4](https://openstax.org/books/principles-marketing/pages/17-1-the-use-and-value-of-marketing-channels) makes availability and intermediary contributions consequential alongside communication. Here those distinctions are used to compare complete distribution arrangements in MKT.11, while SYSE.8 and ECO.3/5 supply the developed provider and exchange constructions. A channel label or a general textbook comparison does not establish a best arrangement for a particular customer situation.

## MKT.Reference:14 - Refresh the affected use

Begin with the changed fact and the decision it could alter. A new customer role, lost permission, unavailable provider, different population, failed causal assumption or changed cash date calls for a different return. Follow the actual dependency to the affected offer, explanation, preparation, assignment or programme. Preserve unchanged local results and tell receivers which condition has changed before their next reliant action.

If a common source claim fails, inspect the methods and applications that depend on it. If only one organisation changes its data conditions, revise that opportunity and the common-work comparison it affects.
